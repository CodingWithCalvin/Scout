# Scout mascot artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Source: `Scout-mascot-source.png` (transparent PNG). Run `scripts/Generate-Icon.ps1` to export `Scout.png` and `Scout.ico` at 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels. The executable and WPF window use the ICO; the titlebar and sidebar use the PNG.

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create a new original mascot application icon for Scout, a Windows app that identifies TV episodes. Single friendly alert dog head and a little neck, wearing a small explorer/scout campaign hat. Warm tan dog with floppy dark brown ears, cream muzzle, bright curious eyes, subtle smile, navy blue scout hat with a light blue hatband and a tiny plain diamond badge, blue neckerchief. Polished modern flat vector-like mascot illustration with bold clean outlines, large simple shapes, restrained minimal shading, warm approachable personality. Face mostly front-on with a slight jaunty tilt. Centered head fills 85 percent of square image; transparent background outside the mascot. Suitable at 32px app icon size. No text, no lettering, no wordmark, no scenery, no magnifying glass, no compass, no extra props. No resemblance to existing cartoon dogs or known branded mascots. One finished icon, no presentation sheet or mockup.

Name-tag edit (built-in image-generation tool): Add a small cream/light-gold rounded rectangular name tag to the blue neckerchief, left of its knot, reading 'Scout' in bold dark navy lettering. Preserve the mascot identity, pose, illustration style, colors and transparent background. No other changes.

Centered hanging-tag edit (built-in image-generation tool): Remove the pinned badge, restore blue fabric, and hang one gold dog ID tag reading 'Scout' from a small visible gold ring beneath the center bandana knot. Keep the bandana, shorten tails only if needed, preserve the mascot identity, hat, pose, palette, style and transparency.
